About Optrist Co

Our Story

Founded in 2002 in Mansoura, Egypt, Optrist Co has grown into a leading Deutz diesel engine spare parts specialist. From the F4L912 to the BF6M and over 50+ engine models, our expertise spans across all Deutz ranges. We hold ourselves to strict German quality standards, partnering with global manufacturing facilities to provide exceptional products.
