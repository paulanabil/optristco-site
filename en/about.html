Our Heritage & Mission

Dedicated to Deutz Excellence Since 2002

Two decades of specialized expertise in diesel engineering, world-class manufacturing partnerships, and dependable supply to industry leaders across the Middle East.

Engineering Trust, Component By Component

Optrist Co was founded in 2002 in Mansoura, Egypt, with a clear and singular focus: to solve the chronic shortage of high-reliability, precision-fit replacement parts for Deutz industrial and agricultural diesel engines.

Over 22+ years of continuous operation, we evolved from a regional parts distributor into an integrated manufacturing and international trading house. By establishing strict quality benchmarks inspired by German engineering standards, we partner with specialized production facilities globally to manufacture parts that match or exceed original specifications.

Today, Optrist Co supports thousands of critical diesel engines powering irrigation pumps, generators, construction excavators, and transport fleets across Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Libya, and throughout the Middle East.
