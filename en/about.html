About Optrist Co

Your trusted Deutz engine specialist since 2002.

Our Story

Founded in 2002 in Mansoura, Egypt, Optrist Co has grown into a leading supplier of Deutz diesel engine spare parts in the Middle East. We specialize in providing components for the F4L912, BF6M, and over 50 other Deutz engine models.

Our commitment is built on German quality standards. We partner with global manufacturing facilities that meet international standards to deliver premium parts that keep your engines running smoothly.

Our Values

  • Quality: German quality standards applied universally.
  • Reliability: Dependable parts for critical operations.
  • Expertise: Deep knowledge of Deutz engines.
